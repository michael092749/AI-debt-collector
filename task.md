Scenario
A consumer is 180+ days delinquent on a $1,000 debt and has ignored previous contact. Your
agent calls them. Close the highest-value agreement they'll actually honor.
Acceptable outcomes, in order of preference:
1. Full payment
2. Highest-amount downpayment + one more payment
3. Settlement (up to 20% off), max 3 payments
4. Payment plan (no discount), over 3 months max, with biweekly, weekly, or monthly
payments
The smallest payment can never be less than 25%.
Requirements
- Any AI voice platform or framework.
- The consumer's proposed amount must be validated and countered by logic outside the
agent, mid-call.
- Define and enforce your own compliance guardrails. Persuasion that relies on threats,
false urgency, or invented consequences fails regardless of how well it converts.
- Log the final agreement wherever you like, we won't evaluate the destination system
